# Code of Conduct

Roveran welcomes learners and contributors of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Be patient with questions, explain technical feedback clearly, and criticize ideas without attacking people.

Expected behavior includes respectful discussion, constructive reviews, credit for others' work and care with private information. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation. They will consider context and severity, explain decisions when appropriate, and protect the privacy of people reporting concerns.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the private contact method on their repository profile. Do not post private details in a public issue. Maintainers are expected to follow this policy and handle reports impartially.
